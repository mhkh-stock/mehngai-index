        Honest limits — please read

        • Your personal rate is your own basket, NOT the official SPI. The official SPI averages 51 items with government weights; your rate uses your quantities and your prices.
        • PBS publishes PDFs only (no official API). Weekly updates here are manual admin entries from each Friday release, so a new week appears here after the admin enters it — not automatically.
        • Prices shown are national averages across 17 cities, not any one city's price.
        • Alerts are in-app only. There are no SMS or WhatsApp alerts; a target flag shows on your basket when you update a price.
        • Your basket is private: it lives only in your browser and is never sent to the server.
