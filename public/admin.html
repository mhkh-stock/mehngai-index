Admin — enter a new SPI week

PBS publishes the weekly SPI every Friday (for the week ending Thursday) as a PDF on pbs.gov.pk — there is no official PBS API. Open the official executive-summary PDF, read the combined SPI and the item prices, and enter them here. Re-posting the same week replaces it; it never creates a duplicate week.

Spending groups (optional — one per line: label, band, spi, week %, year %)

Item prices — one per line: name, unit, price now, price last week, price last year

Tip: copy the item table from the PDF's price annex. Prices are national averages. Leave a price blank if the release does not give it — that item will simply show no value for that date.
